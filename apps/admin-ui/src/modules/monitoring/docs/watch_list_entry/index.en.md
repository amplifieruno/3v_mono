# Watch-list entries

**Monitoring → Watch-list entries** lists every entry of every watch list:
the **Watch list**, the **Person** (profile) or the **Identity** it names
(one of the two), the **Reason**, when it **Expires** and whether it is
**Active** now. The views **Active** and **Expired** split them; filter by
list to see one list's entries.

Entries are added where their subject is chosen: on a list's **People** or
**Identities** tab, or on a profile's or an identity's **Watch lists** tab.
Here you change an entry's reason and expiry, or delete it. See
*Watch lists*.
