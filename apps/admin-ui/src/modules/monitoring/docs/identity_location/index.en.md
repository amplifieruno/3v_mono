# Locations

A **location** is one step of a person's path: the area entered or moved to,
or the door passed, when, and the camera whose detection placed the person
there. **Monitoring → Locations** lists every step newest first; filter it by
**Area**, **Access point**, **Camera**, **Identity**, **Movement** or time to
see who passed a place.

**Movement** says what the step was: **Entry** (the first place of a session,
or a door passed inwards), **Exit** (a door passed outwards), **Movement** (to
another area, or through a two-way door) or **Stationary** (an estimated
place). A step's page links its detection and its tracking session.

The locations of one session are its path (the session's **Path** tab).
Locations are written by the tracking pipeline only; the list is a log that
loads 50 at a time.
