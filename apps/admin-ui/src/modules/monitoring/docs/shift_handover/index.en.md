# Shift handovers

**Monitoring → Shift handovers** is where one operator passes the shift on
to the next.

## Handing over

1. **+ Create**, choose who takes over (**To**, an active member other than
   you), the shift's start and end, and write the **Summary**: what happened,
   what is pending, what to watch. You are recorded as **From**.
2. On the handover's page, **Add open incidents** links every incident still
   being handled (open, acknowledged or investigating) in one step.
   Incidents already on the handover are skipped, so run it again just before
   you leave to catch new ones. **+ Create** in the incidents list adds one
   incident at a time; delete a row to take one off.

Until it is acknowledged, only you (the sender) can change the handover, its
incidents or delete it.

## Taking over

The views **Waiting for me** and **For me** show the handovers addressed to
you. The handover's page lists **Incidents handed over** with each incident's
title, **status now** and severity, so you see at a glance which
are still open (an incident resolved since shows as resolved). Open one to
handle it.

**Acknowledge** confirms you have read the handover and taken the shift
over. Only the recipient can acknowledge; acknowledging again changes
nothing. From then on the handover is the shift's record: nobody can change
it or its incidents.
