# Who is where and occupancy now

**Monitoring → Who is where** lists each person tracked in a facility right
now: the innermost **Area** they were last seen in, since when (**Entered**)
and when **Last seen**. A person has one row per facility; it moves as they
move and goes when their session ends. The list reads again every 15 seconds
while it is open; pause it from the toolbar.

## Occupancy now

**Monitoring → Occupancy now** shows the facility chosen in the sidebar
(choose one first; "All facilities" shows nothing here):

- **On site now**: everyone in the facility, each person once.
- The **floor plans**, one at a time (the buttons above the drawing choose
  the plan): every area with a shape is coloured by how full it is: empty
  (grey), occupied without a capacity (blue), under half full (green), half
  full or more (amber), nearly full (orange), over capacity (red, outlined in
  red). Each area is labelled with its people against its capacity.
- **Areas**: every area of the facility as a tree with the same figures; an
  area counts the people in its sub-areas too, and says how many are in the
  area itself.
- **Who is where**: the rows above for this facility.

The figures are read again every 15 seconds. They are the same counts as the
facility's **On site now** and the areas' **Occupancy** elsewhere in the
console: a person whose identity was merged into another counts once.
