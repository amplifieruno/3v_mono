# Operator workspace

**Monitoring → Operator workspace** is the page an operator keeps open on
shift. It follows the facility chosen in the sidebar and shows four panels:

- **Live cameras**: the live wall of the cameras running recognition, each
  with the faces it detects as they happen.
- **Open incidents**: incidents still being handled (open, acknowledged,
  investigating), newest first. Open one to acknowledge or handle it.
- **Latest events**: the newest events of critical severity, or of the
  severities the workspace names for this panel (the seeded workspaces name
  medium and above).
- **Occupancy**: the facility's people on site, its capacity, the areas over
  capacity and the busiest area now, read again every 15 seconds (the time
  of the last read is shown under them). Choose a facility in the sidebar
  first.

## Arranging the page

Which panels the page shows, and in which order, is yours: **Arrange this
page** at the bottom lists your workspaces. Create one (or open it and
**Edit**) and, under **Panels**, untick a panel to hide it and move the
others up or down. The **Default** workspace lays the page out; making
another one the default takes it from the previous one. Without a workspace
the page shows every panel in the order above.

Workspaces are personal: nobody else sees or changes yours, and yours do not
change anyone else's page.
