# Processing activities

The **record of processing** (GDPR Art. 30) lists what the organization does
with personal data. **Compliance → Processing activities** holds one entry per
activity, such as "Face recognition in common areas" or "Physical access
control".

Each activity states:

- its **Name** and **Purpose**, and how it works (**Description**);
- its **Legal basis**: consent, contract, legal obligation, vital interests,
  public task or legitimate interests (Art. 6(1));
- the **Retention policy** that decides how long its data is kept. A policy an
  activity names cannot be deleted; deactivate it, or point the activity at
  another policy first;
- its **Owner**, an active member accountable for it;
- whether it is still **Active**.

## Data classes

The **Data classes** tab lists the classes of personal data the activity
handles, the same classes retention policies keep and purge (detections,
sessions, face templates of withdrawn consent, the audit log and so on). Add
one with **+ Create**; each class appears once per activity. A class is removed
with its row's **Delete**; it is never edited.

## Impact assessment (DPIA)

**DPIA** says where the data protection impact assessment (Art. 35) stands:
not required, required, in progress or approved. Attach the assessment as the
**DPIA document** (PDF or Word, up to 20 MB). It is an ordinary organization
file, opened through a link. Saving an approved DPIA without its document shows
a warning. The **DPIA to do** view lists the activities whose
assessment is required or in progress.
