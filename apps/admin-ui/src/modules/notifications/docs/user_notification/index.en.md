# Inbox

The **Inbox** holds your in-app notifications: what notification rules,
escalation steps, your own subscriptions and ready reports told you on the
in-app channel. Only you see your inbox; other members have their own.

## The bell

The bell in the header counts your **unread** notifications and lists the
latest five; **Open the inbox** leads here. It is read again every 30
seconds, and at once after you mark something read or acknowledge it.

## Read and acknowledged

- **Mark read** takes a notification off the bell's count. It stays in the
  inbox.
- **Mark all read** on the list marks every unread notification of yours
  read. It never touches another member's inbox.
- **Acknowledge** says you have seen it and are on it; it is read as well,
  and the delivery log shows the acknowledgement.

Acknowledging an **escalation page** (a notification an escalation step sent
about an incident nobody acknowledged) also acknowledges that incident, as
long as it is still open and unacknowledged: the escalation stops and the
incident's timeline names you. An incident someone else acknowledged, or one
already resolved or closed, is left as it is. A notification from a rule or
a subscription never changes its incident.

## Views

- **Unread**: what the bell counts.
- **Not acknowledged**: what you have not taken on yet.
