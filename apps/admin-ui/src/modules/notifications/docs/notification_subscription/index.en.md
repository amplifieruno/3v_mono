# My subscriptions

A **subscription** is your own standing order to be told about events. Only
you see your subscriptions; every member keeps their own under **My
subscriptions**.

## What it follows

Choose at least one filter. Every filter you set must match, the others are
left open:

- **Event type**: only events of one type, such as a blacklist match.
- **Facility**: only events in one facility.
- **Person**: only events about one known person, through any of their
  identities.
- **Identity**: only events about one tracked face, for someone without a
  profile.
- **Watch list**: only watch-list matches of one list.

**At least** sets the least severe event you are told about: **Info** for
every event, **High** for high and critical events only.

## How you are told

**Channel** is in-app (your inbox, the bell in the header), email, SMS or
push, at the address of your account. A subscription never uses a webhook:
other systems are notified through webhook subscriptions. During your quiet
hours only critical events reach you at once; the rest wait for the quiet
hours to end.

## Unsubscribing

Switch **Active** off to unsubscribe. Nothing more is sent, and the
notifications already sent stay in the **Deliveries** tab and the delivery
log. Switch it on again to resubscribe. Subscriptions are not deleted, so
their history is kept; change the filters instead to follow something else.
