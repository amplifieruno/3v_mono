# Identity duplicates

**People → Identity duplicates** is the review queue of pairs of identities
whose faces are so alike they may be the same person. Each pair shows its two
identities, the **Similarity** of their face templates (up to 1 for
identical), when it was **Found** and its **Status**: **Open**, **Merged** or
**Not a duplicate**. The **Open** saved view shows the pairs waiting for a
decision.

## Comparing

A pair's page shows the two identities side by side: the photo, status,
profile, and what each holds (photos, face templates, watch-list entries, open
duplicate pairs, pending verifications).

## Merging

**Merge identities** asks which identity to **Keep** (usually the one linked
to a profile, or with more photos); the comparison marks it and says what
moves and what closes. After a review and a confirmation the other identity is
merged into the kept one, which then opens. The pair becomes **Merged** and
links the merge. Identities linked to two different profiles cannot be merged;
the comparison says so before you send. A merge can be rolled back from
**Identity merges**, which reopens the pair.

## Dismissing

**Not a duplicate** records that the two are different people; nothing
changes on the identities. **Reopen** puts a dismissed pair back in the queue.
