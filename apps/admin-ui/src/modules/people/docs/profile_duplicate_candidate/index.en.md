# Profile duplicates

**People → Profile duplicates** is the review queue of pairs of profiles that
may describe the same person, for example two profiles with the same email or
employee ID. Each pair shows its two profiles, a **Similarity** from 0 to 1,
what it **Matched on**, when it was **Found** and its **Status**: **Open**,
**Merged** or **Not a duplicate**. The **Open** saved view shows the pairs
waiting for a decision.

## Comparing

A pair's page shows the two profiles side by side: name, type, status, email,
phone, company, employee ID, department, job title and dates, with the fields
that differ marked, and what each profile holds (identities, documents,
consents). A restricted or erased profile is called out under the comparison.

## Merging

**Merge profiles** asks which profile to **Keep** (usually the older or more
complete one); the comparison marks it and says what moves. After a review
and a confirmation:

- the identities, documents and consents of the other profile move to the
  kept one, and its watch-list entries fold into the kept profile's;
- the other profile becomes **inactive** (it is not deleted);
- its open duplicate pairs close as **Merged**;
- the kept profile's latest biometric consent decides face matching for every
  identity it now has.

There is no undo. A pair where either profile is **erased** cannot be merged
(dismiss it instead), and neither can one where processing of either profile is
**restricted**: lift the restriction first. Both are refused before anything is
sent.

## Not a duplicate

**Not a duplicate** closes the pair without changing either profile.
**Reopen** puts a dismissed pair back in the open queue.
