# Identity relationships

A relationship records that two identities are related: **Companion**
(usually seen together), **Related** (family, a colleague or another known
tie) or **Other**, with a **Confidence** from 0 to 1 and a **Note**.

Relationships are added from an identity (**More → Add relationship**) and
listed on the **Relationships** tab of both identities. A pair is stored once,
whichever identity it was added from, so adding the same two again is
refused. The relationship's own view (**Open** on a row) shows both
identities; **Edit** changes the relation, confidence and note, and
**Delete** removes the relationship only. Relationships change nothing about
matching; investigations and analytics read them.
