# Companies

**Companies** in the Organization section lists the companies people belong
to. A person's profile names their company, and a visit can name the company
the visitor comes from.

A company's page has two tabs: **Details**, and **People**, the profiles that
belong to the company (code, name, type, status and whether a face is linked).
**+ Create** there starts a new profile already set to this company.

## Kinds

- **Tenant**: rents space in the business center. **Suite** is the office or
  unit it occupies.
- **Contractor or vendor**: works on site for a limited time, for example
  maintenance or construction. **Cost center** is the code it is billed under.
- **Service provider**: runs a service of the building itself, such as
  security or cleaning.
- **Visitor organization**: the organization visitors come from, used to
  group visits.

The **Tenants** and **Contractors and vendors** views in the list toolbar
filter by kind; the second also includes service providers. The search box
looks in the name, the contact email and the cost center.

## Status and deleting

A company that no longer works with the organization is set **Inactive**. Its
people and visit history stay as they were.

A company that profiles or visits still point at cannot be deleted: the
server refuses it. Set it inactive instead, or move its people to another
company first.
