# Moves between areas

A row counts how many times, on one day of a facility, people went **from** one
area straight **to** another on the same visit: the second area is the next one
they were seen in. The day is the facility's calendar day of the arrival in the
second area.

These counts feed the **Movement between areas** matrix on the Analytics page,
summed over the chosen period, and the **abnormal movement** event rules, which
compare a person's move with the facility's last 30 days.
