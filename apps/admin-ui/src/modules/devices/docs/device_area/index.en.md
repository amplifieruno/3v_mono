# Covered areas

A **covered area** is an area of a facility that a device also sees, besides
the area it is installed in (its own **Area**). A camera on a landing that
looks down a corridor, for example, covers both.

Covered areas are listed, added and removed on the device's **Areas** tab, and
shown on the device as **Also covers**; they have no page of their own. An
area must belong to the device's facility and is listed once per device. To
change one, remove it and add the other. An area that a device still covers
cannot be deleted.
